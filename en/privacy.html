2. Core principles

  • Official Tesla path only: Vehicle data comes from Tesla’s official OAuth / Fleet API after you authorize the App. We do not collect or store your Tesla password.
  • On-device by default: Telemetry, drives, charges, and tracks are stored in an encrypted local database on your phone unless you separately opt in to cloud features.
  • Optional cloud needs separate consent: Cloud collection, backup/sync, and in-app family sharing require additional express consent.
  • No ad tracking: No advertising SDKs, no sale of personal information, no cross-app tracking inside the App.

3. China compliance & App Store standards

6. Purposes & legal bases

Core archive features; maps; optional cloud fill-in & backup; family sharing; Cloud entitlement checks; customer support; security/compliance. Marketing unrelated to these purposes and sale of personal information are not practiced. Under PIPL, bases include consent, contract performance, and statutory obligations as applicable.

7. On-device by default & optional cloud

Default: encrypted local DB on your device. Uninstall/wipe/device backup deletion removes local data.

Optional cloud (Cloud membership): requires Cloud Collection Disclosure consent; used for continuous logging, sync, and backup; not for ads; you may stop and delete remote copies anytime.

Retention: local until you delete; cloud copies (if enabled) in mainland China, typically rolling ~365 days, deleted when cloud is turned off; support email generally ≤24 months unless law requires longer.

8. Maps & location services

For lawful maps and reverse geocoding in mainland China, TeLog may use Amap (Gaode) Open Platform with keys obtained by the operator. Coordinates and render params may be sent to Amap; Tesla passwords/tokens are not. Optional OSM Nominatim fallback may be used when allowed and Amap is unavailable.

9. Third parties

Tesla (Fleet API), Apple (App Store / StoreKit), Amap, China-region cloud hosts for token proxy / optional vault, optional OSM Nominatim, and services you configure (MQTT / Home Assistant). We do not sell or rent your personal information beyond those uses and legal requirements.

10. Cloud membership & Apple payments

Cloud membership (IDs typically com.jchen.Telog.plus.monthly / com.jchen.Telog.plus.yearly; also called TeLog Plus / Cloud) is billed by Apple. We do not collect card numbers. Refunds/invoices follow Apple.

Free examples: live status, emergency tools, VIN/tires, recent drives/charges, basic stats, standard share cards, OTA history, TOU electricity settings. Cloud examples: always-on smart logging, full history, deep dashboards, battery health/value, attribution & driving reports, Vehicle Passport, family share, expense, road-trip check-ins, geofence/parking, sync/backup, PDF/Excel, premium share templates. App screens and App Store listing control.
