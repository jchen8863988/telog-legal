TeLog · Cloud collection

Cloud collection data processing notice

Version 2 · Effective: 8 July 2026

This notice applies when you separately opt in to TeLog cloud collection (24/7 recording). When off, vehicle data stays on your iPhone only. When on, the categories below may be uploaded to encrypted servers in mainland China.

What we collect

Trips, location, charging, battery state

Trip routes and position points, charging sessions and power curves, vehicle online/sleep/drive/charge state, battery level and range, odometer readings, and related geofences and address labels.

  • drives, positions
  • charging_processes, charges
  • state_events, cars
  • geofences, addresses

Purpose

24/7 recording to fill gaps when the phone misses data

Continuous encrypted backup for trip stats, charging analysis, battery health, and gap-fill. Not used for ad targeting or sold to third parties.

Storage & retention

China · encrypted · deleted when you turn off

Stored on encrypted servers in China; default retention 365 days; remote copy deleted when cloud collection stops.

How to withdraw

Turn off anytime; remote copy deletion requested

Turn off cloud collection in Settings → Advanced, or use Privacy & data → Stop cloud & delete remote copy.
